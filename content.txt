*Our Process*

*From Scrap to Recovered Metals*

*01 — Sourcing and Assessment*  
We assess the material type, condition and composition to establish suitability and agree commercial terms.

*02 — Sorting and Processing*  
Scrap is sorted and processed using dismantling and separation methods suited to its construction and material characteristics.

*03 — Metal Recovery*  
Copper, aluminium and ferrous components are separated and organised into saleable material streams.

*04 — Supply to Industry*  
Recovered metals are supplied to trade and industrial buyers against agreed material descriptions and specifications.

*Recovered Metals*

*Copper*  
Recovered copper, including winding wire, offered according to the available grade and condition.

*Aluminium*  
Aluminium recovered from housings, castings and other components, sorted according to material type.

*Ferrous Metals*  
Recovered iron and steel components supplied for further recycling and processing.

Material availability, composition and commercial terms are confirmed for each lot.
