Privacy Policy

Learn how Ashiga AI Solution collects, safeguards, and handles your information.

Effective Date: November 5, 2026 | Last Updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Ashiga AI Solution ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is dedicated to safeguarding any personal or business data you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines our procedures regarding the collection, use, disclosure, and protection of information obtained through our website, communications, and B2B metal recycling services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you submit an inquiry, request an assay evaluation, partner with our recycling operations, or communicate with our representatives. Such data may include:

  • Contact Details: Full name, corporate email address, business telephone number, and company name.
  • Commercial Inquiry Data: Descriptions of scrap metal streams, volume estimates, alloy specifications, and logistics delivery requirements.
  • Technical & Website Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, pages visited, and timestamps gathered via standard server logs for website performance optimization.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process collected information solely for legitimate commercial, operational, and regulatory purposes, including:

  • Evaluating metal scrap compositions and delivering accurate recycling quotations.
  • Executing B2B sales orders, logistics transportation, and secondary raw material fulfillment.
  • Responding promptly to customer inquiries, scheduling facility audits, and providing customer support.
  • Complying with statutory environmental reporting, anti-waste trafficking regulations, and tax requirements.
  • Maintaining website security and preventing fraudulent inquiries.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or lease your corporate or personal information to third parties. Information may only be disclosed to trusted third-party service providers (such as freight carriers, accredited assay testing laboratories, or statutory customs and environmental authorities) solely to the extent necessary to perform authorized transactions.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate physical, electronic, and managerial safeguards to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no data transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed completely secure, and you acknowledge that transmissions are made at your own discretion.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal and commercial inquiry records only for as long as necessary to fulfill the operational purposes outlined in this policy or as mandated by applicable commercial and environmental laws in India and international trade jurisdictions.

7. Your Rights

Depending on applicable regulations, you have the right to request access to the information we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate data, or request the deletion of your personal contact records where retention is no longer legally necessary.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological, legal, or operational developments. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
